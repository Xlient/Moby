# Delete your Moby account

You can delete your Moby account and its data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Moby and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Privacy & data**.
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Without the app

Email **[CONTACT EMAIL]** from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Moby account".
We'll delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your account (email, name, sign-in).
- Your settings, saved areas, alert preferences and phone push tokens.
- The reports you sent, including your notes and their exact locations.
- The record of which alerts were sent to your phone.

## What is kept

- Public alerts that your reports helped confirm stay up, without any link to you.
- Copies in our backups are removed within **14 days**.

See the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) for details.
