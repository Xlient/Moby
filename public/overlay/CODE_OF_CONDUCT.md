# Code of Conduct

Moby follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the people
who rely on Moby. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Xlient/Moby/security/advisories/new) or by contacting
[@Xlient](https://github.com/Xlient). Reports are handled confidentially.
