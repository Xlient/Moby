# Sponsoring Moby

Moby is free, open source and built by one independent developer. The live service costs money every
day it runs: hosting on Nebius, model calls for translations and explanations, and official-site search.
Sponsorship keeps alerts flowing for everyone, including the people who could never pay for them, and
funds new regions, the Bluetooth mesh and offline features.

**[Sponsor Moby on GitHub →](https://github.com/sponsors/Xlient)**

## Tiers

Monthly sponsorships through GitHub Sponsors. Each tier includes everything in the tiers above it.

| Tier | Per month | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Supporter** | $5 | Your name in this file, and a thank-you in the next monthly update. |
| **Backer** | $25 | A monthly update on what shipped and what's next, and early-access Android builds before each public release. |
| **Partner** | $100 | Your logo (small) in the README and here, and a 30-minute feature-request call each quarter. |
| **Lead sponsor** | $500 | Your logo (large) at the top of the sponsors section in the README, a monthly call, and one feature request a quarter considered first in the roadmap. |

One-time sponsorships of any amount are welcome too, and are thanked in the next monthly update.

### What we promise, and when

| Promise | Tier | When |
|---|---|---|
| Name added to this file | Supporter and up | Within 7 days of sponsoring |
| Thank-you post | All | Within 7 days of sponsoring |
| Monthly update | Backer and up | By the 5th of each month |
| Early-access build | Backer and up | At least 3 days before each public release |
| Logo in the README | Partner and up | Within 14 days of sponsoring |
| Feature-request call | Partner (quarterly), Lead sponsor (monthly) | Offered at the start of each period |

**What sponsorship never buys:** a say in which warnings are shown, how alerts are ranked or labelled, or what
the safety guidance says. Official warnings and the trust rules stay the same for everyone, and Moby has no ads
and doesn't sell data. Feature requests are considered, not guaranteed, and must keep to Moby's
[principles](CONTRIBUTING.md#principles-please-dont-break-these).
